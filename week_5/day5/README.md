# W5D5 — AI-Powered EDA on Indian Business Dataset

## Objective

Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) on an Indian business dataset to understand revenue, business categories, cities, employees, and customer ratings.

## Dataset

The dataset contains **100,000 synthetic Indian business records** with 7 columns:

* `business_id` — Unique business ID
* `business_name` — Business name
* `city` — Indian city
* `category` — Business category
* `revenue` — Business revenue
* `employees` — Number of employees
* `rating` — Customer rating

## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn

## EDA Performed

The analysis includes:

1. Dataset size and column inspection
2. Data type analysis
3. Missing-value analysis
4. Statistical summary
5. Revenue analysis by city
6. Revenue analysis by business category
7. Average customer rating by category
8. Revenue distribution and outlier visualization

## Visualizations
